2. Overview and legal bases

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, for example a name, an email address or an IP address. We only process it to the extent permitted by law. Depending on the purpose, we rely on:

  • your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR),
  • the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR),
  • a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR),
  • our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Where we access information stored on your device or store information there, § 25 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) also applies.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Beispiel-Hoster GmbH, Hosterstraße 1, 12345 Hosterstadt. When you visit the website, the host processes technically necessary access data on our behalf. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the host. The servers are located in Germany.

Each time the website is accessed, the following data is automatically recorded in log files (server logs):

  • IP address of the requesting device (shortened/anonymised, if configured that way),
  • date and time of the request,
  • page or file requested and amount of data transferred,
  • status code of the request (for example “successful” or “not found”),
  • the website you came from (referrer), if your browser transmits it,
  • browser type, browser version and operating system.

We and the host process this data to deliver the website, to ensure its stability and security and to defend against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in secure and error-free operation. The log data is deleted or anonymised after 7 days. The data is not combined with other data sources.

4. No cookies, no analytics or tracking tools

This website does not set any cookies itself, does not use web analytics or tracking services or advertising networks, and does not store any information in your device’s browser (neither cookies nor local storage or session storage). We therefore do not need a cookie banner. The only exception is the optional Twitch livestream, which is only loaded after your explicit consent (see section 9). Your choice of language (German/English) is not stored either; it is only part of the page address (parameter “lang”).

5. Fonts

The fonts on this website are delivered from our own server or come from your device. No connection to Google or other font providers is made.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, phone or via our profiles on social networks, we process the data you provide (for example name, contact details and the content of your message) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering enquiries). We delete the data once the request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Please note: unencrypted emails can be read by third parties in transit. Do not send confidential information unprotected.

7. Inquiries, orders and shipping

Inquiries about our pieces and the purchase process take place outside this website, by email or direct message. In doing so we process the data required for handling, usually name, address, contact details, the goods ordered and payment details. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Statutory retention obligations under commercial and tax law (in particular § 257 HGB and § 147 AO, usually six to ten years) are based on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

For delivery we pass your name and address to the shipping company DHL (Deutsche Post AG). For payment we use bank transfer / PayPal; the payment service provider receives the data required for payment.

As our products are only sold to adults, we check the buyer’s age of majority before the sale, based on the information provided in the course of the order. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the applicable youth protection regulations.

9. Twitch livestream (only with consent)

On this website you can watch the livestream of our Twitch channel directly. The stream is not loaded automatically. As long as you have not consented, no connection to Twitch is made; instead you see a locally stored placeholder image.

What happens after you consent?

When you click “Load livestream”, your browser loads a script and the player from Twitch (player.twitch.tv) and connects to Twitch’s servers. In doing so, Twitch receives at least your IP address, information about your browser and device, the page visited and playback data. Twitch may use cookies or similar technologies and access information on your device. If you are logged in to Twitch at the same time, Twitch may be able to assign the visit to your account. According to its own information, Twitch also processes this data for its own purposes (for example security, product improvement and advertising) and is the controller for that. We have no influence on this further processing.

Provider and transfer to third countries

The provider is Twitch Interactive, Inc., 350 Bush Street, 2nd Floor, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA. Twitch names Twitch Sweden AB (Gothenburg, Sweden) as its representative in the European Union pursuant to Art. 27 GDPR; please refer to Twitch’s privacy information for the exact address. Your data may be transferred to the USA. According to Twitch, this takes place on the basis of appropriate safeguards such as data transfer agreements; where the recipients are certified, the EU-US adequacy decision (Data Privacy Framework) is also relevant. In the USA, according to the assessment of the Court of Justice of the European Union, a lower level of protection than in the EU may exist in individual cases and authorities may access data. By giving your consent you also expressly agree to the transfer of your data to the USA (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR), where such a transfer takes place.

Legal basis, withdrawal, storage

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: click “Withdraw consent and remove stream” below the player, or reload or close the page. This disconnects the connection to Twitch. The lawfulness of processing carried out up to the withdrawal remains unaffected. We do not store your decision; when you visit again, we ask you again. You can delete data already stored at Twitch and adjust settings there: Twitch privacy information.

12. Your rights

You have the following rights against us as the controller:

  • access to the data we store about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR) and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To exercise them, please contact us at the contact address above.

13. Right to object and withdrawal of consent

If data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to the processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (see section 9).

14. Complaint to a supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: The State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg), Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
